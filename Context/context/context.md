# Project: EXTREME DEPT KIDZ – UI/UX Elevation

## Brand Identity
Extreme Dept Kidz is a **premium kids fashion e-commerce brand** that stands for **quality, playful confidence, and modern luxury**. The target audience is **parents seeking elevated, stylish clothing for children** that feels refined, warm, and trustworthy. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

## Core UI/UX Vision
We want an **ultra sleek, neat, and premium experience** with:
- **Glassmorphism** accents (subtle frosted glass surfaces)
- **Fluid interactions & micro-animations**
- **Clean typography & whitespace**
- **Warm, heart-friendly visuals**
- **Fast performance & seamless navigation**
- A style grounded in **Figma-inspired layout harmony**

Design language: **Minimal + Elegant + Gentle + Playful + Highly Functional**

## Must-Keep Functional Requirements
- Navigation (Shop, Boys, Girls, New Arrivals, Collections)
- Customer Care links (Track Order, Contact, Size Guide)
- Footer with social icons
- Shop filters (price, category)
- Cart and checkout must remain intact

## Avoid Breaking
Do *NOT* remove any:
- Shopping or cart functionality
- Customer care or footer
- Security indicators (SSL, payment badges)
- Existing content hierarchy
