# 🍪 How to Clear admin-token Cookie on Mac & iPad

## 🖥️ Mac - Safari

### Method 1: Clear Specific Cookie (Recommended)

1. **Open Safari**
2. **Open Developer Menu:**
   - Go to **Safari** → **Settings** (or **Preferences**)
   - Click **Advanced** tab
   - Check ✅ **Show Develop menu in menu bar**
   - Close Settings

3. **Open Web Inspector:**
   - Go to **Develop** menu → **Show Web Inspector**
   - Or press **Cmd + Option + I**

4. **Find Cookies:**
   - Click **Storage** tab (left sidebar)
   - Expand **Cookies**
   - Click on `https://extremedeptkidz.com`

5. **Delete admin-token:**
   - Find `admin-token` in the list
   - Right-click → **Delete**
   - Or select it and press **Delete** key

6. **Refresh page:**
   - Press **Cmd + R** or click refresh

---

### Method 2: Clear All Site Cookies (Safari)

1. **Open Safari**
2. **Safari** menu → **Settings** (or **Preferences**)
3. Click **Privacy** tab
4. Click **Manage Website Data...**
5. **Search** for: `extremedeptkidz.com`
6. Select it and click **Remove**
7. Click **Done**
8. **Refresh** the page

---

## 📱 iPad - Safari

### Method 1: Clear Specific Site Data

1. **Open Safari**
2. **Go to Settings app** (iPad home screen)
3. **Safari** → Scroll down
4. **Advanced** → **Website Data**
5. **Search** for: `extremedeptkidz.com`
6. **Swipe left** on the entry
7. Tap **Delete**
8. **Go back** to Safari and refresh

---

### Method 2: Clear All Cookies (iPad Safari)

1. **Settings** app
2. **Safari**
3. Scroll down to **Privacy & Security**
4. Tap **Clear History and Website Data**
5. Confirm **Clear History and Data**
6. **Go back** to Safari

---

## 📱 iPad - Chrome

### Method 1: Clear Site Cookies

1. **Open Chrome**
2. **Tap three dots** (⋮) in top right
3. **Settings** → **Privacy and security**
4. **Clear browsing data**
5. Select **Cookies and site data**
6. **Time range:** Last hour
7. Tap **Clear data**
8. **Go back** to Chrome

---

### Method 2: Site Settings (Chrome iPad)

1. **Open Chrome**
2. **Go to:** `https://extremedeptkidz.com`
3. **Tap lock icon** (🔒) in address bar
4. **Site settings**
5. **Cookies** → **See all cookies and site data**
6. Find `admin-token`
7. **Swipe left** → **Delete**
8. **Go back** and refresh

---

## 🔄 Quick Method: Private/Incognito Mode

**Mac (Any Browser):**
- **Safari:** **File** → **New Private Window** (Cmd + Shift + N)
- **Chrome:** **File** → **New Incognito Window** (Cmd + Shift + N)
- **Firefox:** **File** → **New Private Window** (Cmd + Shift + P)

**iPad:**
- **Safari:** Tap tabs icon → **Private** (bottom)
- **Chrome:** Tap three dots → **New Incognito Tab**

**Note:** Private mode starts with no cookies, so you can test login fresh.
